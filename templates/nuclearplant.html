The SIS trip always protects the core by inserting rods, with or without self-healing. Without self-healing, the valve stays shut and the reactor stays safely offline until an operator forces a manual restart. With self-healing, a validated backup pump engages once the trip is confirmed, and the valve only reopens autonomously once the thermal interlock clears.

Training lab only. All values, thresholds, and the reactor model itself are entirely fictional and arbitrary — this is not based on, and should not be read as representing, any real reactor design, safety limit, or thermal-hydraulic physics.
